How to use the result

Do not size only to the average: the peak day governs the desk count. Use managed team anchor days and booking soft caps to keep attendance near the managed pattern; hold a small overflow option and review real check-in data after 8 weeks before rightsizing.

Inputs still needed from a customer: real headcount by office, working-week and holiday rules, hybrid policy and enforcement, check-in/booking data, collaboration-space demand, and lease constraints.
